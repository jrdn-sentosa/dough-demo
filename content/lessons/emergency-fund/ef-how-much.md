The right size for your fund starts with your **essentials**: what you'd need to get by for a month.

## What to count

Rent, food, phone, transportation, and anything else you can't skip. If your housing or meals are prepaid, count only what you still pay each month. Leave out extras like streaming, eating out, and shopping.

## Your goals, step by step

1. **One month of essentials.** This is the starting goal. It's a realistic place to start, and it covers many common surprises.
2. **Three months.** A stronger cushion for longer gaps, like losing a job or a bigger medical bill.
3. **Six months.** Worth considering if your income changes a lot from month to month.

## Start where you are

Dough! picks a starting goal for you based on your answers, and you can change it any time. If you already have some savings set aside, it can count toward the goal so your loaf starts partway risen.

Progress matters more than perfect. A small fund you actually build beats a big goal you never start.
